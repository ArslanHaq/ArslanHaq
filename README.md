## Featured project: XcelTube

**[xceltube.com](https://www.xceltube.com)** is an exam-preparation platform for students in Pakistan (MDCAT, ECAT, NUST and more), with video lectures, past papers, practice tests and an AI tutor. I'm the lead developer and wrote most of the code across the web app, the NestJS API and the admin panel. The code is private.

### AI Tutor

- **Chat tutor** on Amazon Bedrock, using Amazon Nova 2 Lite by default, with streamed answers and maths rendered with KaTeX
- **Voice tutor** with Amazon Nova 2 Sonic speech-to-speech streaming, live captions and Urdu script support
- **Understands uploads:** students can attach images and PDFs of questions or notes
- **Context-aware:** knows the subject, chapter, lecture or past paper it was opened from, with a topic picker for each subject
- **Study shortcuts:** one tap to solve faster, remember a concept or check an answer
- **Tutor personas**, saved conversations with automatic titles, and summaries that keep long chats within limits
- **Usage limits** for guests and free plans (daily prompts, images and PDFs, plus per-minute rate limits). Guest chats carry over after sign-up.
- **Admin controls:** switch and test models, manage tutors, subjects and study tools, review usage and conversations, and preview prompts

### Learning platform

- Courses organised into subjects, chapters and video lectures, with watch history and progress tracking
- Past papers, full-length mock tests and a custom practice-paper builder that draws from the question bank
- Quizzes with attempts and results, a leaderboard and question reporting
- Subscriptions with Easypaisa payments, promo codes and referrals
- Downloadable resources, blogs, an exam-updates feed with RSS, and a careers page
- SEO landing pages for exams such as MDCAT, ECAT, NUST, CSS and ISSB, plus MDCAT, ECAT and NUST aggregate calculators
- Accounts with email OTP, Google sign-in, authenticator-app two-factor authentication and push notifications
- An admin panel for courses, questions, past papers, pricing, users, reviews and content analytics
